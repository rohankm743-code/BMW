04 / ENGINEERING

RACE-BRED ARCHITECTURE.

01.0

3D-PRINTED HEAD & FORGED CRANK

The cylinder head features a 3D-printed core that allows coolant passages to be routed in geometric shapes impossible with traditional metal casting. This optimizes thermal reduction around the combustion chambers, enabling a compression ratio of 9.3:1 under 2.1 bar of boost.

  • Closed-deck design withstands over 140 bar peak cylinder pressure
  • Twin mono-scroll turbochargers with electronic wastegates
  • Track-proven dry-sump inspired oil return chamber
02.0

M CARBON RACING BUCKETS

Designed for high-g lateral forces exceeding 1.3g. The structural shell is molded from pure carbon fiber reinforced plastic (CFRP) with cut-outs for multipoint racing harnesses and removable headrest cushions for helmet clearance on circuit days.

  • 9.6 kg weight saving versus standard M Sport seats
  • Integral pneumatic side bolster adjustment
  • Illuminated M4 badging integrated into carbon shell
03.0

ACTIVE M DIFFERENTIAL & xDRIVE

An electronically controlled multi-plate clutch continuously modulates torque distribution between the rear wheels from 0% to 100% lock in milliseconds. In available M xDrive models, drivers can decouple the front axle completely with the push of a button for 100% pure rear-wheel-drive drift freedom.

  • Integrated 10-stage M Traction Control slip settings
  • Active M Differential integrated into dynamic stability algorithms
  • Four 100mm titanium-finished sport exhaust tailpipes
04.0

TORSIONAL RIGIDITY & ADAPTIVE DAMPING

A network of underhood and underbody shear plates, strut-to-tower carbon braces, and aluminum subframe ties elevate torsional rigidity to racecar standards. Continuously variable electromagnetic valves in the Adaptive M Dampers adjust in microseconds according to road sensor telemetry.

  • Aluminum front shear panel directly bolted to side members
  • CFRP lightweight carbon fiber roof lowers center of gravity
  • Dual-rate electric power steering with variable M rack ratio
